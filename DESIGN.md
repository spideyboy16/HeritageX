## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital sanctuary for cultural exploration, positioning itself at the confluence of modern curatorial prestige, cinematic storytelling, and archival scholarship. The intended audience spans international cultural connoisseurs, art historians, luxury cultural travelers, and discerning digital citizens seeking depth over brevity.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily on **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** combined with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- **Curatorial Restraint**: Negative space is intentional and generous, functioning like the hushed gallery halls of high-end institutions.
- **Cinematic Submersion**: Deep, soot-warm obsidian canvases allow polychromatic temple art, stone carvings, woven textiles, and archival photography to project outward with lifelike luminance.
- **Craftsmanship & Materiality**: Subtle hairline borders, gilded edge glints, and tactile surface tiers echo antique brass joinery, patinated parchment, and sandstone masonry without slipping into heavy skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around low-glare, warm-dark tones reminiscent of museum light chambers and aged architectural stone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted antique gold. Used for key focal points, active states, active tab indicators, and distinguished metadata stamps. Supported by `#E5C378` for interactive highlights and `#8C6D37` for subdued decorative framing.
- **Secondary (`#B85D3A`)**: Sun-baked terracotta. Reserved for editorial accents, rare collection indicators, curatorial quotes, and critical focal flags.
- **Tertiary (`#2C3540`)**: Archival indigo-slate. Acts as a balancing cool anchor in deep visual hierarchy, tag surfaces, and subtle contrast backplates.
- **Neutral Foundations**:
  - `Canvas Ground`: `#0C0B0A` (Deepest soot-black)
  - `Surface Base`: `#141312` (Warm charcoal)
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#1C1A18` (Sandstone-tinted dark ash)
  - `Surface Raised`: `#252320` (Light-retaining tier)
- **Text & Reading Layers**:
  - `Primary Text`: `#F6F3EE` (Warm ivory)
  - `Secondary Text`: `#D8D2C5` (Soft linen)
  - `Muted / Caption`: `#9E9689` (Aged stone dust)
  - `Subtle Outlines`: `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.16)` (Gilded hairline borders)

## Typography

The typography unites traditional Indian scholarly dignity with modern Swiss structural legibility.

- **Headlines & Display (Playfair Display)**: Delivers an authentic editorial feel reminiscent of museum monographs, cultural journals, and gallery plaques. Ligatures and delicate serifs are maintained with high vertical rhythm. Display headings feature slightly tighter tracking to amplify authority.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures maximum legibility under high-contrast dark environments. Its geometric balance offsets the expressive cadence of the serif headlines.
- **Labels & Archival Metadata**: Styled in uppercase with wide letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to simulate curatorial inventory codes, cataloging timestamps, and architectural provenance markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a classic 12-column editorial grid with deliberate asymmetrical pacing for featured artifacts and monolithic full-bleed hero sections.

- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column structure with `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Narrative exhibits frequently span 8 columns offset by a 4-column curatorial commentary rail.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column structure with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Dynamic sidebars collapse into sticky bottom trays or expanding overlay drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column structure with `1.25rem` screen margins and `1rem` gutters. Storytelling cards adopt full-width immersive vertical scrolls.

Generous vertical rhythm (`space-xl` and higher) takes priority over information density, allowing visual assets breathing room akin to physical artifacts mounted in spotlighted display cases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on intense dropshadows, which wash out dark interfaces. Instead, spatial priority is defined by **Tonal Layering**, **Hairline Luster**, and **Amber Ambient Glows**:

1. **Level 0 (Canvas Backplane - `#0C0B0A`)**: The infinite gallery background. Contains no borders or highlights.
2. **Level 1 (Submerged Panels - `#141312`)**: Primary card bodies, collections, and catalog lists. Outlined with a microscopic 1px border of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Cards - `#1C1A18`)**: Interactive focus state. Border luminescence increases to `rgba(229, 195, 120, 0.28)` accompanied by a subtle ambient radiance: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Curatorial Drawers & Floating Navigation - `#252320`)**: Elevated over a deep dark backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) brightness(60%)`). Features a distinct gilded inner top rim: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(229, 195, 120, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances precision with softness. 

- Base components (inputs, tags, standard buttons) leverage `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`).
- Primary content containers, archival showcase cards, and interactive tiles utilize `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- High-level modals, hero media wrappers, and curatorial feature plaques use `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).

Sharp edges are systematically softened to avoid stark digital harshness, yet curvature is kept disciplined to retain an architectural, stone-chiseled aesthetic rather than a casual, bubbly app demeanor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Curatorial Action**: Background filled with Antique Gold (`#C5A059`), text in Canvas Ground (`#0C0B0A`, font-weight 600). Hover triggers a transition to Sandstone Glow (`#E5C378`) with a warm diffusion aura.
- **Secondary Ghost / Bordered**: Transparent background with a 1px border in `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`. Text in Warm Ivory (`#F6F3EE`). Hover brings a soft fill of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
- **Text Link Button**: Displayed in Soft Linen (`#D8D2C5`) with an underline offset of 6px, transitioning to Antique Gold on hover.

### Chips & Archival Badges
- Constructed with `0.5rem` radius, padded at `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.
- Background set to `#1C1A18` with subtle borders (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.16)`).
- Labels are all-caps `label-sm`, tracking wide (`0.12em`), tinted in `#C5A059` or Terracotta (`#B85D3A`) for rare period classifications.

### Cards & Artifact Showcases
- **Editorial Exhibition Card**: Comprises high-fidelity 4:5 or 16:9 imagery framed inside a `1rem` curved container. Uses an organic dark-gradient vignette at the base of the image to merge text overlays cleanly into the ivory typography.
- **Catalog Metadata Grid**: Alternates subtle tonal backgrounds (`#141312` and `#1C1A18`) with hairline dividers (`rgba(216, 210, 197, 0.08)`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Low-profile inputs with `#141312` base fill, bordered in `rgba(216, 210, 197, 0.15)`.
- Focused state transitions the border to antique gold (`#C5A059`) with a slight inner glow. Placeholder text rests in Stone Dust (`#9E9689`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox (`0.25rem` radius) and circular radio elements framed in antique gold hairlines. Checked state fills with `#C5A059` and displays a deep `#0C0B0A` geometric check icon.

### Curatorial Storytelling Components
- **Provenance Inspector**: A split-screen timeline module showing historical chronology via a vertical gilded thread (`#C5A059` at 30% opacity) connected with glowing anchor nodes.
- **Audio Tour Bar**: A floating pill player anchored to the bottom screen edge, surfaced in frosted obsidian glass (`#141312` at 85% opacity, `blur(16px)`), framed with a single-pixel gold gradient rim.
